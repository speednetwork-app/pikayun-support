隐私政策

马斯克加速器 · 最后更新：2026 年 11 月 17 日

简要说明：你可以通过邮箱注册或登录，查看账号概览和订阅地址，并选择开启 VPN。 注册不会自动购买套餐；iOS 商城可在开放时通过 Apple 订阅。不含广告、第三方统计或用户追踪。 本说明适用于 1.6.5 及以后移除 IP 查询功能的版本； 1.6.4 及更早版本隐私说明仍适用于相应旧版本。

一、我们收集哪些信息

如果你主动使用「我的」页面注册或登录，我们会处理你输入的 邮箱、密码及必要的验证信息，以创建账号、防止滥用或完成身份验证；后端要求邮箱验证时，会向你提供的邮箱发送验证码。登录后会获取该账号的套餐状态、每日流量趋势、有效期、使用限制和订阅地址。密码只通过 HTTPS 发送至账号服务器， 不会保存在设备上。本 App 不要求姓名、电话号码，也不会读取通讯录、相册、位置权限或 设备标识符。

二、数据如何被使用

App 在你主动注册、登录、使用 VPN 或订阅套餐时处理提供服务所需的数据，数据流向如下：

数据去向用途
邮箱、密码、验证信息和可选邀请码（仅在主动注册或登录时） api.pikayun.com 创建账号、防止滥用、发送邮箱验证码或验证已有账号；密码不会保存在本机
登录凭证、账号概览、流量趋势和订阅地址 api.pikayun.com 保持登录状态，并向已登录用户显示账号信息、系统公告和订阅地址
Apple 签名交易凭证、交易标识、商品、币种和标价、有效期、续订／退款状态，以及随机生成的账号绑定标识 Apple 与 api.pikayun.com 验证购买、绑定马斯克加速器账号、开通与恢复订阅、同步续订和撤销；不获取 Apple ID 密码或银行卡资料

本版本不提供 IP 查询和地图功能，不向 IP 数据库或地图服务发起相应请求。

仅在你登录并主动开启 VPN 后，App 才会获取所选线路的连接配置，并使用 Apple Network Extension 建立 VPN。 连接期间，设备网络流量会通过你选择的线路传输，可随时从首页断开；不读取设备 GPS。

三、数据存储在哪里

旧版本曾保存在设备上的查询记录，本版本不再读取、新增或上传，也不会因移除功能自动删除。 卸载 App 不会取消 Apple 订阅或删除服务端交易记录。

登录成功后，登录凭证和账号邮箱保存在 iOS 钥匙串中，以便保持登录状态。密码不保存在 本机。套餐状态、流量趋势、有效期和使用限制会安全缓存于本机，以便网络异常时继续显示； 订阅地址不会写入该缓存。你可以在「我的」页退出登录， 删除本机保存的登录凭证、邮箱和账号缓存。用于恢复中断购买的最少操作记录（账号邮箱、随机操作编号、交易关联编号和待补报的操作结果）另行保存在钥匙串中，按账号隔离；不保存密码、签名交易凭证或付款资料，确认删除账户后会清除该账号的本机操作记录。复制订阅地址后，内容将进入系统剪贴板，并受系统剪贴板 机制管理。1.6.5（33）及之后的构建不再展示或提供复制订阅地址的入口。

服务端保存必要的订阅绑定、验签结果和交易记录，用于开通、恢复、对账及处理退款争议；收到的 Apple 签名通知加密保存。购买尝试、取消、待确认及验证结果会记录，用于恢复中断购买和防止重复下单。交易资料不用于广告追踪。退出登录或卸载不会删除这些服务端资料，也不会自动停止续费；你可以在 Apple 的订阅设置中管理或取消续订。

选择记住同一线路的风险确认后，账号与线路配置的摘要保存在本机钥匙串中，不保存该线路密码或完整配置，不上传服务器。 退出登录后保留，删除该账号时清除。1.6.5（33）及之后的构建可在「我的 → 隐私与数据」清除；1.6.5（32）的入口为「关于与帮助 → 连接诊断」。记住确认不代表服务器证书已验证。

1.6.5（35）起，兼容连接弹窗提供可选的「记住当前账号所有 HY2 线路的风险确认」，默认不勾选。仅在你主动勾选并继续后，本机才保存账号摘要和确认范围；此确认允许当前账号在本机跳过所有现有、以后新增或配置变更的 HY2 线路的服务器证书校验，不再逐条询问。不会保存或上传线路密码、完整配置；旧版逐条确认不会自动升级为全部线路确认。可在「我的 → 隐私与数据」清除，删除该账号时也会清除。流量加密不等于服务器身份已验证，仍存在服务器被冒充或中间人攻击的风险。

本机仅保留最近一次 VPN 连接的阶段、时间、错误分类和收发计数，24 小时后过期。 诊断不记录密码、订阅链接或访问的网站地址。1.6.5（33）及更早构建不自动上传；1.6.5（32）提供主动复制给技术支持的入口，1.6.5（33）起不再展示该诊断面板。

1.6.5（34）起的可选连接失败诊断：默认关闭。只有在你明确启用后，App 才会在 VPN 连接失败或意外断开时，将随机排查编号、线路编号、协议、App 版本与构建号、固定失败阶段和错误分类、耗时、兼容模式、可选系统原因码及至多 8 条固定握手事件代码，经已登录 HTTPS 接口发送至 api.pikayun.com，供技术支持排查。不上传原始错误文本、账号邮箱、用户或设备标识、密码、令牌、完整配置、订阅地址、访问内容、目标地址或收发流量计数。接口通过现有登录凭证验证上报权限，但诊断记录不写入账号标识；现有 API 的认证及访问日志机制不因此改变。该诊断不用于广告或跨应用跟踪，不出售或向第三方提供 VPN 服务数据。

不启用仍可使用 VPN，可在「我的 → 隐私与数据」随时关闭。失败上报在当前登录会话内限量、短暂重试，最多等待 15 分钟；退出登录、删除账户或关闭设置会清除尚未发送的队列。网络不可用或 App 终止时可能无法送达。服务端在受限目录中最多保留 7 天，随后清理；未保存账号关联，技术支持使用 App 显示的随机排查编号查找。

账户删除

登录后在「我的 → 删除账户」发起删除，核对说明、重新输入密码并确认。删除不是仅退出登录或暂时停用：将删除登录账户、邮箱和个人资料、非必要用量明细、工单内容，撤销全部设备的登录凭证与原订阅链接，终止该账号的剩余套餐使用权限。必要财务订单、交易资料及防重复认领标识按适用要求保留，以处理对账、退款和争议，不用于重新开通已删除账号或广告跟踪。数据清理遇到暂时故障时会重试，并在删除结果中说明。

删除账户不自动取消 Apple 订阅，也不自动退款。删除前请使用页面提供的 Apple 订阅管理入口取消不再需要的续订。尚有余额、佣金或正在处理的付款时，可在同一页面提交删除申请并查看进度；不会自动清零，结算完成后自动执行删除，无需重新申请。

Privacy Policy

马斯克加速器 · Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short: You may optionally register by email or sign in to view your account and subscription URL, and choose to use VPN. Registration does not purchase a plan. The iOS store offers Apple subscriptions when enabled. The app contains no ads, third-party analytics or user tracking. This notice applies to version 1.6.5 and later, without IP lookup. The previous notice for version 1.6.4 and earlier continues to apply to those older versions.

1. Information We Collect

If you choose to register or sign in on the Account screen, we process your email address, password and necessary verification information to create an account, prevent abuse or authenticate an existing account. When the server requires email verification, it sends a code to your email address. After authentication, the app retrieves account information and the subscription URL. The password is sent over HTTPS and is not stored on the device. The app does not require your name and never accesses contacts, photos, location permission or device identifiers.

2. How Data Is Used

When you choose to register, sign in, connect to VPN or subscribe, the app processes the data needed to provide those services:

DataSent toPurpose
Email, password, verification information and optional invitation code (only when you choose to register or sign in) api.pikayun.com Create or authenticate an account, prevent abuse and send verification codes; the password is not stored locally
Authentication credential and subscription URL api.pikayun.com Keep you signed in and display your subscription URL
Signed Apple transactions, transaction identifiers, products, currency and price, expiry, renewal/refund status and a random account-binding identifier Apple and api.pikayun.com Verify, link, activate and restore subscriptions, and reconcile renewals or revocations. We do not receive your Apple ID password or payment card details.

This version does not offer IP lookup or maps and does not make the corresponding IP database or map-service requests.

Only after you sign in and choose to connect does the app obtain the selected line's configuration and establish a VPN using Apple Network Extension. While connected, device network traffic passes through that line. You can disconnect from Home at any time. The app does not read device GPS.

3. Where Data Is Stored

This version does not read, add to or upload any lookup records retained locally by older versions. Removing the feature does not automatically erase those records. Deleting the app does not cancel Apple subscriptions or remove server-side transaction records.

After a successful sign-in, the authentication credential and account email are stored in the iOS Keychain to maintain the session. Your password is not stored. The subscription URL is fetched after sign-in and is not written to the account cache. Signing out from the Account screen deletes the locally stored credential and email. If you copy the subscription URL, it is placed on the system clipboard and handled by the operating system's clipboard mechanisms. Build 1.6.5 (33) and later no longer displays or offers a copy action for the subscription URL.

Minimal interrupted-purchase records (account email, random operation references, transaction correlation references and pending operation results) are separately stored in the Keychain, isolated per account. They do not contain passwords, signed receipts or payment details. Signing out retains these records for recovery; confirmed account deletion clears that account's local records.

We retain necessary server-side subscription bindings, verification results, purchase attempts and transaction records for activation, restoration, reconciliation, duplicate prevention and refund disputes. Received signed Apple notifications are encrypted at rest. These records are not used for advertising tracking. Signing out or deleting the app does not remove server-side records or cancel renewal. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple's subscription settings.

If you choose to remember a line's risk confirmation, a digest of the account and line configuration is kept in the local Keychain, not the line password or full configuration. It is not uploaded. It survives sign-out, is removed when that account is deleted, and can be cleared in Account → Privacy & Data in build 1.6.5 (33) and later. In build 1.6.5 (32), use About & Help → Connection Diagnostics. Remembering confirmation does not mean the server certificate was verified.

Build 1.6.5 (35) adds an optional, unchecked-by-default choice to remember risk acceptance for all HY2 lines for the current account. Only checking it and continuing saves an account digest and scope on this device. This explicitly allows skipping server-certificate verification for that account's existing, future and changed HY2 configurations without per-line prompts. No line passwords or full configurations are stored or uploaded. Earlier per-line choices are not automatically expanded. Clear it in Account → Privacy & Data; deleting the account also clears it. Encryption does not authenticate the server, and impersonation or interception remains possible.

Only the most recent VPN connection's phases, times, error categories and transfer counters are retained locally, expiring after 24 hours. Diagnostics do not record passwords, subscription URLs or visited website addresses. In build 1.6.5 (33) and earlier they are not uploaded automatically. Build 1.6.5 (32) lets you choose to copy them to technical support; build 1.6.5 (33) removes that panel.

Optional connection-failure diagnostics from build 1.6.5 (34): Off by default. Only after you explicitly enable it, failed VPN connections or unexpected disconnections send a random report ID, node record ID, protocol, app version/build, fixed failure phase and error category, elapsed time, compatibility mode, optional system reason and up to eight fixed handshake event codes to api.pikayun.com over the authenticated HTTPS API for technical support. Reports exclude raw errors, email, user/device identifiers, passwords, tokens, full configuration, subscription URLs, traffic content, destination addresses and traffic counters. Existing login credentials authorize submission but account identifiers are not written into diagnostic records. Existing API authentication and access-log handling are unchanged. Diagnostics are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. VPN service data is not sold or provided to third parties.

You may use VPN without enabling diagnostics and disable them at any time in Account → Privacy & Data. Bounded retries remain in the current sign-in session for at most 15 minutes; sign-out, account deletion or revocation clears unsent reports. Delivery may fail without network access or when the app terminates. Server records stay in a restricted directory for at most seven days before cleanup. No account association is stored; support locates reports using the random ID shown in the app.

Account deletion

After signing in, open Account → Delete Account, review the consequences, re-enter your password and confirm. This deletes the login account, email, personal profile, nonessential usage details and support content, revokes all device sessions and the old subscription link, and ends remaining service access. Necessary financial orders, transaction records and anti-replay identifiers are retained under applicable financial and dispute requirements, not to recreate the account or track you for advertising. Temporary cleanup failures are retried and reflected in the result.

Account deletion does not cancel Apple subscriptions or automatically issue a refund. Use the subscription-management link before deleting. Accounts with a balance, commission or an in-flight payment can submit a deletion request and check its progress in the app. Funds are not automatically forfeited; deletion completes automatically after settlement, without a second request.

4. Network Services

  • api.pikayun.com — registration, verification, sign-in, account data, subscription URL and user-requested VPN configuration
  • Apple — in-app purchases, subscription management, transaction verification and server notifications

5. What We Do Not Do

  • No advertising or ad SDKs
  • No third-party analytics, telemetry or crash-reporting SDKs; only the optional minimal connection-failure diagnostics described above
  • No user tracking; we never request App Tracking Transparency permission
  • We do not sell account data or provide authentication credentials or subscription URLs to advertisers; account data supports account, VPN and subscription services
  • Registration does not purchase a plan; Apple subscriptions require your confirmation on Apple's purchase screen
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions requested; iOS asks you to approve adding a VPN configuration when first setting it up

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
