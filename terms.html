本 App 继续采用 Apple 标准最终用户许可协议（EULA）。 本页说明现有功能和订阅使用方式，不修改或替代 Apple 标准 EULA；品牌改名不改变原有账号、商品 ID 或已经取得的订阅权益。

一、账号与 VPN 服务

你可以注册或登录账号，选择线路并主动开启或断开 VPN，查看套餐、用量与有效期。注册不会自动购买套餐。 1.6.5 及以后的版本不再提供 IP 查询或地图功能。连接状态不等于所有目标网站都可访问；请以实际网络情况为准。

二、Apple 自动续订套餐

App 提供每月 500GB 或 1500GB 流量的月付、年付自动续订套餐。月付按月计费，年付按年计费。 年付套餐按订阅生效日每月重置所选流量额度，不一次性发放全年流量。

具体可购买商品、币种、价格、计费周期和生效日期，以 App 内 Apple 商品信息及系统购买确认页为准。 本页不写死价格，也不表示尚未生效的调价已生效。购买需由你在 Apple 系统购买窗口确认。

三、恢复购买与管理续订

可在 App 商城使用「恢复购买」。购买会与原服务账号关联，恢复时请使用原购买账号。 自动续订由 Apple 管理；可前往 iPhone 或 iPad「设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅」管理或取消， 也可查看 Apple 的取消订阅说明。

退出登录、卸载 App 或删除服务账号不会自动取消 Apple 订阅，也不会自动退款。 如需退款，可通过 Apple 问题报告页面提出申请，处理结果以 Apple 为准。

四、隐私与联系

数据用途、保存、连接诊断和账户删除说明，请阅读 隐私政策。 技术支持：info@robinads.agency。

Terms & Subscription Information

马斯克加速器 · Last updated: 16 November 2026

The app continues to use the Apple Standard EULA. This page explains existing features and subscriptions; it does not amend or replace that agreement. Rebranding does not change existing service accounts, product IDs or acquired subscription entitlements.

1. Accounts and VPN

You can register or sign in, choose a line, explicitly connect or disconnect VPN, and view your plan and usage. Registration does not purchase a plan. Version 1.6.5 and later does not provide IP lookup or maps. A connected status does not guarantee reachability of every destination.

2. Auto-renewable Apple subscriptions

Monthly and annual plans provide either 500GB or 1500GB each month. Monthly plans are billed monthly; annual plans are billed annually. Annual-plan data resets monthly based on the subscription's effective date, not as a single full-year allowance.

Available products, currencies, prices, billing periods and effective dates are shown in the app's Apple product information and system purchase confirmation. This page does not hardcode prices or imply a scheduled price change is already effective. Purchases require your confirmation in Apple's purchase sheet.

3. Restore and manage

Use Restore Purchases in the store with the original service account. Apple manages automatic renewal. Manage or cancel under Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad; see Apple's cancellation instructions.

Signing out, uninstalling the app or deleting the service account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription or issue a refund. Request a refund through Apple's Report a Problem service; the outcome is determined by Apple.

4. Privacy and support

See the Privacy Policy for data use, storage, local diagnostics and account deletion. Contact: info@robinads.agency.
